# Product Guide: Arthana

# Initial Concept
Arthana is a dynamic personal finance management SaaS designed to act as an "Automated Pocket Financial Advisor." It goes beyond simple expense tracking by providing proactive, tactical allocation advice and automated financial safety net calculations based on a user's risk profile.

# Vision & Overview
Arthana is a dynamic personal finance management SaaS designed to act as an "Automated Pocket Financial Advisor." It goes beyond simple expense tracking by providing proactive, tactical allocation advice and automated financial safety net calculations based on a user's risk profile.

## Target Audience
- Public users seeking automated, proactive financial management.
- Individuals looking for a "Spartan Mode" or tactical allocation advice when nearing a deficit.
- Users who want their emergency fund targets automatically calculated based on their employment and risk profile.

## Core Features (MVP)
1.  **Dynamic Budgeting:** Automated allocation of income into specific categories based on customizable percentages.
2.  **Auto Emergency Fund:** Dynamic calculation of emergency fund targets (3, 6, or 12 months) based on employment and risk profile during onboarding.
3.  **Smart Advisory & Anomaly Detection:** Automated notifications for rapid budget depletion with one-click actionable recommendations.
4.  **Transaction Logging:** Real-time income and expense tracking synced with remaining category budgets, fully localized in Indonesian.

## Product Objectives
- **Proactive Guidance:** Provide actionable advice to prevent monthly deficits.
- **Automation:** Minimize manual tracking and calculation effort.
- **Security:** Ensure strict data isolation and protection for user financial data.
- **User Engagement:** Deliver a modern, accessible interface that feels "alive" and helpful.
